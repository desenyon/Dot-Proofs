## Queue

1. **Collatz conjecture.** For every integer n ≥ 1, there exists an integer k ≥ 0 such that C^k(n) = 1, where C(n) = n/2 for even n and 3n + 1 for odd n. Standard (unaccelerated) map only. [Tao, Forum of Mathematics, Pi 10 (2022), e12](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2022.8). Tao's theorem gives, for each f(n) tending to infinity, min_k C^k(n) < f(n) for a logarithmic-density-one set of n; this neither fixes the target to 1 nor handles every n. [Bařina (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-025-07337-0) reports finite verification for n < 2^71. No accepted universal proof identified. Status: open.
2. **Erdős–Straus conjecture.** For every integer n ≥ 2 there are positive integers x,y,z, not required to be distinct, with 4/n = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z. For Lean use the cross-multiplied equality 4xyz = n(xy+xz+yz), with positivity preserved. [Salez (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.6307) describes a modular sieve and finite checking through 10^17; this is not modular coverage of all inputs. Status: open in sources checked. At stage 2 reconstruct prime reduction from its scaling witnesses; then test which residual classes the sieve leaves and check later reports before claiming a best bound.
3. **Hadwiger's exact coloring conjecture.** For every integer t ≥ 1 and every finite simple undirected graph G with no K_t minor, G has a proper vertex coloring using at most t−1 colors. A minor permits vertex/edge deletions and edge contractions; do not substitute topological minors or list coloring. [Delcourt–Postle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.01633) gives O(t log log t) coloring, not exact t−1. Review [September 2026 update](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06867) and [October 2026 list variant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01946) before recording current best bounds; the linear list variant is not equivalent. **Status: external pre-anchor disproof; Dot-Proofs replay and exact statement audit pending.** The [September 23, 2026 counterexample](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/157.md) addresses ordinary chromatic number, not the list or plane-coloring variants. Keep ID 3 and its frozen stage 3 position for a bounded provenance and replay audit, not as an open proof-search target.

4. **Frankl’s union-closed sets conjecture.** Let ℱ be a finite family of finite sets whose union is nonempty, such that A∪B belongs to ℱ for every A,B in ℱ. There exists an element of the union that belongs to at least half the members of ℱ. [Gilmer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09055) established a positive constant bound; [Yu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00658) gives a numerically evaluated bound around 0.38234 via optimization. [Costa–Sadhu (October 2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02295) report a universal lower bound of 0.3828852549667978 and an upper ceiling near 0.3828852599667 for their particular inequality, not for the conjecture. No settlement identified in the checked sources. Audit their numerical supplement if pursuing this route; their inequality alone does not reach one half.

## Stage 1, entry 1: bounded descent obstruction

For q ≥ 1, the first three steps from 4q+1 are 12q+4, 6q+2, and 3q+1 < 4q+1. This excludes that residue as the least counterexample. The initial attempt to generalize to a uniform time-to-first-descent fails. In fact, for an integer B ≥ 1, take x = 2^(B+1)−1. At even times 2j for 0 ≤ j ≤ B, the orbit equals 3^j·2^(B+1−j)−1. Inductively the displayed value is odd for j < B, so two ordinary steps give (3y+1)/2 = 3^(j+1)·2^(B−j)−1; the intervening odd-time value 3y+1 exceeds y. The even-time values strictly increase, since y becomes (3y+1)/2. Therefore no time 1 through 2B descends below x. This refutes a uniform bound on first descent, not Collatz. The finite script tested q ≤ 4096 and B ≤ 512 without contradiction, and n=27 demonstrates failure within 20 steps.

Artifacts: CollatzStage1.lean (formalization of modular reduction and finite obstruction; see verification log), check_collatz.py and stage-1-evidence.json. Do not call these new results about the full conjecture. Next changed approach: allow first-descent length to depend on the parity prefix. Compute exact affine residue classes and exclude the rising 2-adic path from any finite termination argument; the remaining issue is uniform coverage over *integers*, not over all 2-adic paths.

## Open-at-start audit: entry 3

The pinned [source challenge](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/lean/ComparatorChallenges/HadwigerCounterexample.lean) states finite witnesses with h(G) < χ(G) and excludes infinite chromatic number. Its placeholders define verification targets, not proof evidence. For the queue formulation take t = h(G)+1. A clique-minor model consists of disjoint nonempty connected branch sets with an edge across each pair. Contract a spanning tree of each branch set and delete unused vertices and edges to obtain K_t. Conversely, track connected original-vertex preimages of surviving vertices through deletions and contractions; a surviving edge has an original edge between its two preimages. Finite vertex count bounds every feasible clique-minor order. Thus a witness has no K_t minor and cannot be colored with t−1 colors. This bridge has been checked on paper, not formalized locally.

Édouard Bonnet's [immutable Lax record](https://github.com/lax-archive/lax/issues/191) and [pinned replay instructions](https://github.com/EdouardBonnet/hadwiger-counterexample/blob/7681499cc08e2ba78b6470d47d2983fdf3fbaed8/README.md) document an external Lean v4.33.0 replay before the scope anchor.
